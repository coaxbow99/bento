Jack-O'-Lantern Halloween Sushi

Ingredients~

  • 2 cups Sushi Rice (cooked and seasoned)
  • 2 tbsp Salmon flakes, minced carrots, or orange tobiko (for the orange color)
  • Nori sheets (seaweed)
  • 1 small Cucumber (sliced into thin strips for the eyes/stem)
  • Soy sauce for serving

Instructions~

  1. Mix your cooked sushi rice with salmon flakes or carrot juice until it turns a vibrant, pumpkin-orange color.
  2. To make the face details, roll small strips of orange rice inside small pieces of Nori to create individual nose and eye shapes.
  3. Place your long cucumber strips along with the tiny eye/nose rolls onto your main sheet of Nori.
  4. Pack the remaining orange pumpkin rice around them tightly to form the full round face shape.
  5. Roll the entire roll tightly in a full Nori sheet, slice it with a wet knife to reveal the hidden Jack-O'-Lantern faces, and serve!
